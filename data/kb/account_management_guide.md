# Account Management Guide

## Password resets
If an automated password reset link fails to arrive or expires, support can perform a manual,
secured password reset after identity verification. A temporary password is then issued through a
secure channel.

## Account lockouts
An account is temporarily locked after 3 consecutive failed login attempts, as a security
precaution. To unlock the account, the customer must complete a password change after identity
verification; this also re-secures the account against further attempts.

## Adding or removing users
A secondary authorized user can be added to an account once their details are collected and
verified. Removing a user, or opting out of promotional communications, can be done at any time by
contacting support or updating communication preferences in account settings.

## Subscription pauses
A subscription can be placed on hold for up to 3 months at a time (for example while travelling).
Billing is adjusted for the hold period and the subscription automatically resumes at the end date
unless the customer cancels beforehand.

## Service upgrades and downgrades
Upgrading from a Basic to a Premium service tier takes effect immediately, with the new billing
rate applied from the next cycle. A confirmation email with the new contract details is sent after
the change.

## Security questions and username changes
Security questions can be updated at any time after two-factor identity verification. Username
changes are permitted as long as the requested username is available and complies with the naming
policy (no impersonation, no offensive terms).

## Account consolidation and transfers
Two separate accounts can be merged onto a single consolidated invoice starting from the next
billing cycle. Account transfer requests can be cancelled/reverted before final processing
completes if the customer reports an error.
