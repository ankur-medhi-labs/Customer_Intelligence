# Escalation and Support Channels

## When a case is escalated
A case is escalated to a senior support specialist when the customer mentions cancelling or
churning, threatens legal action, reports severe or repeated unresolved failures, or describes
financial or safety harm. Escalated cases are reviewed within 1 business hour.

## Contact channels
Customers can reach support via the in-app chat, the support email address, or the phone support
line. In-app chat and phone support are available during business hours; email support responses
are typically sent within 1 business day.

## Formal complaints
A formal complaint raised by a customer is logged with a unique reference number (format
SVS-XXXX) and forwarded to the relevant team (e.g. the logistics partner for delivery complaints)
for review and follow-up. The customer is notified of the outcome once the review is complete.

## Compensation and goodwill gestures
Loyalty discounts, goodwill credits, and partial fee waivers are offered at the discretion of the
support specialist for long-tenured customers or as compensation for confirmed service errors.
These are one-time gestures and are not guaranteed to recur.
