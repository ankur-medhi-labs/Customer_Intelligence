# Billing and Refunds Policy

## Duplicate or incorrect charges
If you are charged twice in a billing cycle or charged an incorrect amount, the duplicate or
incorrect amount is refunded to your original payment method within 5 business days once
confirmed. You will receive an email confirmation once the refund is processed.

## Late fees
A late fee applies if a payment is not received within 5 days of the invoice due date. If a
payment was made before the fee was applied but the fee still appears on your statement (a
processing delay), the late fee is waived and refunded automatically once the payment record is
verified - no need to dispute manually.

## Payment deferral
Customers who need extra time to pay an invoice can request a one-time 7-day extension on the due
date per billing cycle. Deferral requests are approved automatically unless the account has an
existing overdue balance older than 60 days.

## Price changes
Standard subscription prices are reviewed annually and may increase in line with inflation (CPI).
Any price change is communicated to customers at least 30 days before it takes effect, along with
a documentation notice explaining the adjustment.

## Subscription cancellation
Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time with immediate effect on future billing; access
continues until the end of the current paid billing cycle, after which no further charges occur.

## Referral credits
Referral credit is issued once the referred sign-up completes their first paid billing cycle. If a
valid referral code did not apply automatically, support can manually verify and apply the credit
(standard referral credit is $25).

## Invoices and receipts
Copies of past invoices and receipts can be re-sent to the email address on file, or a hard copy
can be mailed to the billing address on request.
