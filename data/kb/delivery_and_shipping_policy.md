# Delivery and Shipping Policy

## Standard delivery timeframes
Standard orders are delivered within 3-5 business days of dispatch. If a shipment is delayed past
the original estimate due to a logistics processing error, the order is fast-tracked and an
updated delivery date is provided along with revised tracking details.

## Address changes
An in-transit order's delivery address can be changed to a different address (for example from
home to a work address) as long as the package has not yet reached the final local delivery hub.
Once updated, the new address is confirmed with the logistics partner and reflected in tracking.

## Damaged or incorrect items
If an order arrives damaged, a replacement is shipped immediately under a new tracking number, and
a prepaid return label is provided for the damaged item. If the wrong product or wrong variant
(e.g. wrong color) is received, a replacement in the correct variant is dispatched immediately and
the customer is given instructions to return the incorrect item.

## Stalled tracking
A tracking status stuck in "Pre-Shipment" for more than 48 hours does not necessarily mean the
item has not shipped - it can indicate a delay in the logistics tracking update rather than the
physical shipment itself. Support can manually confirm shipment status with the logistics partner.

## Shipping fees
Standard shipping is free over the minimum order threshold; expedited shipping carries an
additional fee based on the shipping method selected at checkout. Fee disputes are reviewed on a
case-by-case basis and a partial credit may be issued as a goodwill gesture.

## Service complaints
Formal complaints about delivery handling (e.g. improper package placement) are logged with a
reference number and forwarded to the logistics partner for review.
