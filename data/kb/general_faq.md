# General FAQ

## Company overview
We provide connected home and subscription services, including internet-connected devices,
streaming content, and ongoing subscription plans, supported by a dedicated customer service team.

## Support hours
In-app chat and phone support are available Monday to Friday, 8am-8pm, and Saturday 9am-5pm
(local time). Email support is monitored 7 days a week with a 1 business day response target.

## App permissions
Our mobile app requests only the permissions required for the features you use - for example,
storage access for downloaded content, or notification access for delivery/billing alerts. Any
new permission request introduced in an app update is documented in that update's release notes.
If you are ever asked for a permission that seems unrelated to the feature you're using and it
isn't mentioned in the release notes, please contact support so it can be investigated.

## Feedback
General feedback and feature suggestions can be submitted through the in-app feedback form or via
email support. Feedback does not receive an individual response but is reviewed by the product
team.
